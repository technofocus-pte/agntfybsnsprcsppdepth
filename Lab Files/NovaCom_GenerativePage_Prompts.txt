==============================================================================
NovaCom Telecom - Supervisor Dashboard
Generative Page prompts for Exercise 13
==============================================================================

Paste each block below into the Generative Page chat panel at the point the
lab guide indicates. Paste one block at a time and wait for the page to
finish regenerating before pasting the next.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 1 - Initial page prompt (Exercise 13, Task "Generate the Page")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Build a supervisor dashboard page for NovaCom Telecom customer service team
leads, using data from the Service Tickets table.

Show the following:

1. A KPI summary row across the top with four cards: Total Open Tickets,
   Total Escalated, Total Critical Priority, and Total Resolved.

2. A priority breakdown section showing a count of tickets grouped by the
   Priority column (Low, Medium, High, Critical) as a horizontal bar chart.

3. An open escalations list below, showing Ticket Number, Ticket Title,
   Customer Name, Issue Category, Assigned Agent and Created Date, filtered
   to rows where Status equals Escalated, sorted by Created Date descending.

Use a clean, modern, card-based layout with a white background and an accent
colour of #0078D4. Make the page responsive.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 2 - Refinement 1: KPI cards (Exercise 13, Task "Refine the Page")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Make the four KPI cards equal width and place them side by side in a single
row with a subtle drop shadow and rounded corners. Give the Total Escalated
card a red accent colour so it stands out from the other three.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 3 - Refinement 2: layout (Exercise 13, Task "Refine the Page")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Place the priority breakdown chart on the left at about 60 percent width and
the open escalations list on the right at about 40 percent width, side by
side, directly below the KPI row. On a narrow screen they should stack, with
the chart above the list.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BLOCK 4 - Optional refinement: category breakdown
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Add a second chart below the existing two sections showing the number of
tickets grouped by Issue Category. Use the same card styling as the rest of
the page.


==============================================================================
IF GENERATIVE PAGES IS NOT AVAILABLE IN YOUR REGION
==============================================================================

The Generative Pages maker experience is only available where the environment
region is United States, United Kingdom, Australia, or Singapore. If the
Generative page option does not appear in your app designer, follow the
fallback steps in Exercise 13 of the lab guide instead. The fallback builds
the same supervisor view using a standard Dataverse chart and dashboard,
which is available in every region.
